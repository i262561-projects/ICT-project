CAMPUS PULSE — VENUE BOOKING CHECKLIST

[ ] Event title and organizer details are ready
[ ] Preferred date and backup date are selected
[ ] Start and end times are confirmed
[ ] Expected attendance is estimated
[ ] Venue capacity and accessibility are considered
[ ] Seating and layout requirements are listed
[ ] Audio-visual and power requirements are listed
[ ] Safety and supervision plan is prepared
[ ] Cleanup and return-of-venue plan is prepared
[ ] Event department confirmation has been received

Do not announce the venue as booked until confirmation is received.
