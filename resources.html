How to book a venue

Plan your event early and contact the event department before announcing a venue or date. Venue confirmation depends on availability and approval.

  1. Choose a suitable space. Consider expected attendance, event type and equipment needs.
  2. Submit a request. Share the preferred date, time, expected audience and setup requirements.
  3. Wait for confirmation. Do not treat a venue as booked until the event team confirms it.
  4. Coordinate setup. Confirm seating, audio-visual equipment, access and cleanup arrangements.

Before you request

Keep a backup date ready and provide accurate attendance estimates. For a proposal, use the Contact & Event Proposal page.

Event guidelines

These general guidelines help keep campus activities organized and safe. Follow any additional instructions issued by the university or event department.

Approval first

Obtain the required approval before advertising, inviting external guests or making bookings.

Respect campus community

Keep activities inclusive, respectful and suitable for the university environment.

Safety and supervision

Plan crowd management, emergency access and responsible supervision for participants.

Leave the venue clean

Protect university property and arrange proper cleanup after the event.
