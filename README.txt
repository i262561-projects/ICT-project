NUCESPHERE — FAST-NUCES ISLAMABAD CAMPUS (IICT FALL 2026)

Six-page student project website. NUCESphere is an independent student project and is not an official FAST-NUCES website.

PAGES
1. index.html — home page (original content and layout preserved; brand/logo updated)
2. news.html — department news page (original content and layout preserved; brand/logo updated)
3. events.html — events verified against official organiser/university notices
4. societies.html / clubs.html — Islamabad societies listed in the FAST-NUCES Student Handbook 2025
5. resources.html — resource centre
6. contact.html — contact and event proposal demo form

IMPORTANT
- Pages 1 and 2 are byte-for-byte preserved from the supplied originals. Their old Campus Pulse text/logo remains by design to honor the instruction not to change their content or layout. Pages 3–6 use the NUCESphere brand.
