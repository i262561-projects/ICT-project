News & Announcements

Sports

Registration for the Annual Sports Gala is now open for all departments. Teams can sign up for cricket, football, badminton and table tennis through the event department office.

Deadline: 25 November 2026. Each team must have a faculty coordinator and a captain who will be the point of contact.

Academic

A one-day workshop on machine learning fundamentals will be held on 20 November. Industry speakers will run live demos and a hands-on session.

Seats: limited to 120 students. Register early through the Contact page.

Cultural

Cultural Night returns on 28 November with music, dance, drama and food stalls representing every region. Student societies are invited to apply for performance slots.

Applications can be sent using the Event Proposal form on the Contact page.

Career

More than 20 companies have confirmed participation in Career Fair 2026 on 10 December. Students will be able to submit CVs, join mock interviews and attend career talks.

Bring printed copies of your CV and your student ID card.

Notice

Societies must now submit venue booking requests at least seven days before an event. Requests will be approved in the order they are received.

Full details are available in the Resource Center under Venue Booking.
